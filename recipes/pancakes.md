# Crepes (Thin Pancakes)

## Ingredients
- 4 eggs (size M or L)
- 60 g sugar (¾ dl)
- 200 g wheat flour (3.3 dl)
- A pinch of salt
- 500 ml milk
- 50 g melted butter

## Instructions
1. Beat the eggs with the sugar.
2. Add the flour, milk and salt. Mix until smooth.
3. Stir in the melted butter.
4. Heat a large frying pan over medium heat. Pour in enough batter to form a thin layer.
5. Cook for a couple of minutes until the underside is golden brown.
6. Flip the crepe and cook the other side.
